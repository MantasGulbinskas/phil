## Process finding: opposite-frame forecasts escape the one-live-row rule

The two Millennium markets each carried TWO live, final, headline-scored
rows in opposite outcome frames:

- 4412199 (OpenAI): `de704a0f5b47` No 0.99 (09-17) and `1e6152a33dff`
  Yes 0.22 (09-21, which superseded the Yes-frame `b33d4a5731f7`). The
  two rows imply P(Yes) 0.01 and 0.22 at once.
- 4383152 (AI lab): `61e11058ff43` No 0.98 and `eda44ccf1473` Yes 0.10.

`core/forecast.py` checks the live-row guard on market_id + outcome, so a
re-read recorded on the other outcome neither gets rejected nor
supersedes. Both reads count in the headline stats as independent rows.
The 09-17 No reads were explicitly made without dedicated research, and
the later Yes-frame reads were the researched revision. They should have
replaced the earlier rows, not joined them.

- My side (playbook edit below): before `forecast.py record`, grep
  forecasts.jsonl for a live open row on the same market_id with ANY
  outcome. If one exists in the other frame, record the new read in THAT
  row's frame (P(No) = 1 - P(Yes)) with `--supersede`.
- Protected side: `journal/proposals.md` asks the operator to make the
  guard key on market_id alone, or to convert frames, for binary markets.
