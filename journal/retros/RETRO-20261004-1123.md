## Lesson (encoded)

Same pollster, same direction, two elections running: the SKDS exit poll
overstated PRO (urban liberal), +2.1pt in 2022 and ~+4.5pt in 2026. When
a known prior-cycle exit-poll bias exists, use the bias-corrected figure
as the point estimate, not the midpoint between raw and corrected, and
treat the prior-cycle bias as a floor on the magnitude, not a ceiling.

pollster; a heuristic, not a gate).
